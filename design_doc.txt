01 · DESIGN APPROACH
One job: from "I need a room" to an enquiry sent, in three steps
This document sets out what Roomigo will look like and how its screens are laid out: the logo, colour scheme, typography,
components and a wireframe for every screen. Every decision is checked against four goals.
Clarity
Price, walking time to
campus and verified status
are visible on every hostel
card without opening it.
Trust
Verified hostels carry a
badge. Every status is
shown with a word and an
icon, never by colour alone.
Speed
Search, open a hostel, send
enquiry. Browsing never
requires an account.
Consistency
One palette, one type scale
and one set of components
on every screen and device.
02 · LOGO
The Roomigo logo is kept exactly as designed
Primary. Navy mark and wordmark, "igo" in
teal. Used on white and Cloud backgrounds.
Reversed. Emerald mark, white
wordmark. Used on Navy and over
photos.
App icon. The mark alone, for the
browser tab icon and small spaces.
What it means: a rounded house holding one emerald dot, which reads as a room, a location pin and a keyhole. Rules: keep clear space of
three times the dot's diameter on all sides; never display the full logo narrower than 96px (use the app icon instead); never recolour, stretch,
rotate or add effects.

04 · COLOUR SCHEME
Navy and Emerald on Cloud
Why this scheme was chosen
Two schemes were compared: Navy and Emerald (from the brand board) and Blue and Amber. Navy and Emerald is the final choice
because 1 these are the logo's own colours, so the logo and the interface form one identity; 2 green signals "verified, safe, go",
which is exactly Roomigo's promise, and it is more distinctive than the blue most apps use; 3 Navy gives very high text contrast
16.521 on white); 4 it leaves amber and red free to mean only "pending" and "error".
